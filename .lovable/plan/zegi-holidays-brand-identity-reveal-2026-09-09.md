# Zegi Holidays Brand Identity Reveal

## Goal
Build a cinematic, high-end presentation experience for Zegi Holidays using the uploaded official logo, brand boards, campaign mockups, and travel imagery. The presentation will feel calm, editorial, and luxurious, with minimal copy and strong visual pacing.

## Story and slide flow
1. Opening reveal — Zegi Holidays logo and “A Journey Beyond Expectations” over a cinematic coastal image.
2. The promise — a restrained statement about journey, escape, and discovery.
3. Brand idea — the logo mark revealed through close crops and elegant motion.
4. Color world — Deep Ocean, Aegean Blue, Coastal Mist, Golden Sand, and Warm Ivory.
5. Visual language — coastal light, tactile materials, horizon lines, and carefully framed destinations.
6. Identity in hand — business cards and premium stationery.
7. Journey system — travel documents and guest touchpoints.
8. Brand in motion — billboard and large-format campaign presence.
9. Digital expression — the brand across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
10. Closing — a quiet full-screen destination image with the logo and brand promise.

## Experience
- Fixed cinematic 16:9 slides with fullscreen presenting, keyboard navigation, overview grid, thumbnails, and a shareable slide URL.
- Slow fades, image reveals, subtle parallax, and restrained gold accents; reduced-motion preferences remain respected.
- Minimal typography, generous negative space, precise alignment, and no invented business claims or metrics.
- Responsive editor view for desktop and mobile, plus a print-friendly presentation view.

## Brand system
- Palette: Deep Ocean `#0D4D5C`, Aegean Blue `#1BA5A6`, Coastal Mist `#69C2BE`, Golden Sand `#C9A770`, Warm Ivory `#F2E9DF`.
- Typography: elegant editorial serif for cinematic headlines paired with a refined, widely tracked sans-serif for labels and supporting copy, matching the uploaded identity’s tone.
- The uploaded Zegi logo will be used as the official brand mark and adapted into the site icon.

## Technical details
- Build the presentation at 1920×1080 and scale it cleanly to each viewport.
- Store uploaded imagery through the project asset system and reference those files directly.
- Add route-specific title, description, social metadata, and accessible image descriptions.
- Validate the finished presentation in the live preview at desktop and mobile sizes, checking every slide for cropping, overlap, legibility, and motion behavior.
